Beyond WHOOP
Six things about a day — sleep, bedtime, strain, a nap, when training finished, days since a rest day — each tested on its own against the next morning's score. Only what survives all three checks is shown: a real effect, an even one, and one that beats doing nothing on days it was never fitted to.
